What We Do.

Five services, one accountable team — from the first site survey to the last drop collected.

— 01

Geological Survey

Before any drilling or construction begins, we carry out geological survey and site reconnaissance to understand what's beneath a piece of land — rock type, soil layers, groundwater indicators and terrain features that shape everything that follows. This is desk study and fieldwork combined, not a guess based on the neighbouring plot.

Our geologists map subsurface conditions using established survey methods suited to Nigeria's varied terrain, from coastal sediments to basement-complex geology further inland. The result is a clear picture of what your site can support, before you commit capital to drilling or building on it.

— 02

Borehole Drilling

Reliable water starts underground. We handle the full scope of borehole drilling — siting, drilling, casing, screening and pump installation — for boreholes serving homes, estates, schools, factories and farms, sized to the yield a site can actually sustain.

Every borehole is drilled with attention to aquifer depth, water quality and long-term yield, so you're not paying to redrill in a few years. You get a handover report showing what's under your land, what was installed, and how to maintain it.

— 03

Industrial Cleaning Services

Tank cleaning, facility sanitation and industrial site maintenance carried out to safety and compliance standards — for storage facilities, processing plants and commercial sites that can't afford downtime or shortcuts.

Our crews are trained in confined-space entry, chemical handling and industrial safety protocol, working to a documented process with proper PPE and equipment — not ad-hoc labor. Every job closes out with a completion report for your compliance records.

— 04

Waste Management Services

Collection, handling and disposal of solid and liquid waste for residential, commercial and industrial clients — managed to reduce risk to people, property and the surrounding environment, not just moved out of sight.

We work to documented waste-handling procedures and current environmental standards, with scheduled collection for ongoing clients and call-out service when a site needs a one-off clearance.

— 05

Biodigester Soakaway

Vacuum-tanker emptying and maintenance for septic tanks and biodigesters — scheduled service for households and facilities, or emergency call-out when a system is at capacity.

Our crews handle extraction and safe off-site disposal in line with environmental and public-health standards, with attention to access, odour control and site cleanup so the job doesn't disrupt your property any more than it has to.
