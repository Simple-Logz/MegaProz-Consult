About MegaProz Consult.

A Nigerian geoscience and engineering firm, built on the ground we work in.

Our Story

We Read The Ground Before We Build On It.

MegaProz Consult was founded on a simple conviction: in Nigeria, more construction and water-access projects fail underground than above it. Bad soil data, guessed borehole depths and skipped investigation cost clients far more than the testing would have.

We're a team of certified geologists and engineers who understand Nigeria's geography — the variation in soil and groundwater conditions from the coastal sediments of the south to the basement complex of the north-central states — and what that variation means for a foundation, a borehole, or a drainage plan.

We've carried projects across residential estates, commercial developments, schools, industrial facilities and infrastructure sites, working directly with property owners, developers, contractors and institutions who need ground truth before they commit capital to a build.

Safety and correct equipment handling aren't a compliance checkbox for us — they're how a drilling crew and a client's site both make it to handover without incident. Every rig operator and field technician on our team is trained to a documented safety standard, and every piece of equipment is maintained and operated the way its manufacturer specifies, not the way it's convenient to.

What we've built is a firm that clients bring back for the next project, and refer to people building near them — because the numbers we hand over are the numbers the ground actually gave us.

What We Stand On

Our Values.

Five things that don't change from project to project, regardless of client, size, or deadline.

01 — Quality

Quality

Work measured against a standard, not just a deadline. Every borehole, test result and structural report is checked before it reaches you, not after.

02 — Safety

Safety

Trained crews, maintained equipment, and no shortcuts on site. Every rig operator and technician works to a documented safety standard, on every job, every time.

03 — Integrity

Integrity

The report reflects exactly what the ground showed us. We don't round numbers up to make a client happy, or down to save time in the field.

04 — Sustainability

Sustainability

Water and land resources are handled responsibly, for the long term. We size boreholes to what a site can actually sustain, not what's easiest to sell.

05 — Innovation

Innovation

Better methods and equipment get adopted, not resisted. We invest in tools that make our findings more accurate, not just more familiar.
