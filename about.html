About MegaProz Consult.

A Nigerian geoscience and engineering firm, built on the ground we work in.

Leadership

Francis Offiong

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Francis Offiong is a UK-trained geoscientist and the founder of MegaProz Consult, which was established out of his deep passion towards delivering environmental solutions that promote sustainability and innovation across Nigeria. His technical grounding and hands-on field experience continue to shape the standards the firm holds itself to on every project.

Under his leadership, MegaProz Consult has partnered with both public and private sector organizations on numerous environmental projects work that has built a lasting legacy for the firm within Nigeria's environmental sector. He remains closely involved in the company's day-to-day work, guiding a growing team of geologists and engineers committed to the same standard of accuracy and accountability he built the firm on.

Our Story

We Read The Ground Before We Build On It.

MegaProz Consult was founded on a simple conviction: in Nigeria, more construction and water-access projects fail underground than above it. Bad soil data costs clients far more than proper testing ever would.

We're a team of certified geologists and engineers who understand Nigeria's geography — from the coastal sediments of the south to the basement complex of the north-central states — and what that means for every foundation, borehole and drainage plan.

We've carried projects across residential estates, commercial developments, schools, industrial facilities and infrastructure sites, working directly with owners, developers and institutions who need ground truth before committing capital.

Safety isn't a compliance checkbox for us — it's how a crew and a client's site both make it to handover without incident. Every technician is trained to a documented standard, with equipment maintained exactly as the manufacturer specifies.

What we've built is a firm that clients bring back for the next project — because the numbers we hand over are the numbers the ground actually gave us.

Our Mission

The Right Data, Before You Break Ground.

To give every client — homeowner, developer, or institution — the accurate site data and reliable execution they need to build with confidence, backed by qualified geologists and engineers who stand behind their findings.

Our Values.

Five things that don't change from project to project, regardless of client, size, or deadline.

01 — Quality

Quality

Work measured against a standard, not just a deadline. Every borehole, test result and structural report is checked before it reaches you, not after.

02 — Safety

Safety

Integrity

Innovation
